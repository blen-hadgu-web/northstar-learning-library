Verification & Architecture Note

How container queries drive component fluidity

Every card slot (.catalog-item and .sidebar-card-slot) declares container-type: inline-size. The internal orientation of .resource-card is powered entirely by @container (min-width: 480px) queries and fluid cqi (container query inline) units. No viewport @media queries control internal card styling.
